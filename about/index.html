Hello!

I'm Eric White

I’m a designer with deep experience building products and brands. I live in Washington, DC now however I grew up in the San Francisco bay area. When I'm not designing I'm either playing soccer, swimming, hiking, painting, or hanging out with my daughter – sometimes all in the same day! Unless it's a nap day. Then I just nap all day.

My Journey

My career has been multi-faceted however I've always had one foot planted firmly in user experience design. I started as an advertising art director in San Francisco and lived there during the first dot-com boom where I cut my teeth on UX, UI, and information architecture.
